About Ethiopic numerals

How do Ethiopic numerals work?

Ethiopic numerals combine units (፩–፱), tens (፲–፺) and hundreds with ፻ (100) and ፼ (10,000) separators — a system related to ancient Greek and Hebrew numbering.
