Income Tax Calculator

Estimate employment income tax with progressive monthly bands.

About the tax bands

Which bands are used?

The calculator applies Ethiopia’s progressive monthly employment income tax bands: 0% up to 2,000 ETB, then 15%, 20%, 25%, 30% and 35% for the highest slice. Pension and other deductions are not included.
