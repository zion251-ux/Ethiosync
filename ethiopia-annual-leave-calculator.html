Estimate annual leave entitlement from years of service.

Annual leave rules

How is entitlement calculated?

Ethiopian employment guidance sets a minimum of 16 working days for the first year of service, increasing by one day for every additional two years of service. Under one year, leave is typically pro-rated by completed months.

Sources, effective date & review

Entitlement follows Article 77 of Ethiopia’s Labour Proclamation No. 1156/2019.
