Ethiopian time system

What is the traditional clock?

Ethiopia commonly uses a 12-hour clock counted from dawn (1 o’clock = 7 AM) and dusk (1 o’clock = 7 PM). The modern East Africa Time zone is UTC+3 year-round with no daylight saving.
