Ethiopian & Biblical Baby Names

Explore 750+ biblical names with meanings, origins, pronunciation and scripture references.

About these names

What is the source of these names?

This directory is built from A Dictionary of Ancient & Beautiful Biblical Names in the Royal Amharic, compiled by Tehetena Girma-Asfaw and co-edited by Ras Iadonis Tafari, presented by the Lion of Judah Society. The entries include anglicised biblical names, Hebrew/Greek/Latin origins, meanings and scripture references.

Why is the Amharic script not shown?

The source PDF encodes Amharic characters with embedded fonts that do not map to standard Unicode text, so the script could not be extracted reliably. The English names, pronunciations, meanings and references are preserved exactly from the source.

How are genders classified?

The source uses several gender markers. Entries marked "male" or "female" are classified accordingly. Entries marked "modern male and female", "traditionally male and female", "male and female" or "unisex" are shown as Unisex.
