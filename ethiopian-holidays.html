How dates are determined

Fixed and movable dates

Enkutatash, Meskel, Genna and Timket use fixed Ethiopian dates. Adwa, Labour Day and Patriots’ Victory Day use fixed Gregorian dates. Siklet, Fasika and related Orthodox observances move with the Bahire Hasab Paschal cycle.

Fasting ranges and weekly fasts

Fasting ranges include both the start and end dates shown. Wednesday and Friday fasts are recurring weekly observances, except during the fifty days of joy from Fasika through Pentecost and on other church-appointed exceptions. Follow your parish for personal observance.

Islamic date confidence

Islamic dates use reviewed overrides where available and otherwise a tabular Hijri estimate. Moon sighting and official announcements can shift a date by one day. Ramadan is always labelled as an estimate until officially confirmed.
