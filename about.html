About EthioSync

Free Ethiopian tools, private by design.

What is EthioSync?

EthioSync is a free collection of Ethiopian calendar tools, Amharic utilities, finance calculators and cultural references — all running entirely in your browser. There is nothing to install, no account to create, and no data ever leaves your device.

The toolbox is organised into four categories:

  • Calendar — Ethiopian calendar view, date converter, age calculator and holidays.
  • Amharic & Language — online keyboard, numbers converter and text tools.
  • Finance & Work — income tax, overtime, annual leave calculators and a live currency converter.
  • Reference — live Ethiopian time and a baby-names directory.

Accuracy & sources

Calendar conversions use Julian Day Numbers and are exact for every valid date, including Pagume leap days. Finance calculators apply commonly published Ethiopian employment guidance and are intended as estimates — always confirm payroll figures with your employer or a professional.

Privacy

EthioSync processes everything client-side. The text you type, dates you enter and numbers you calculate never leave your browser. We do not use cookies for tracking, and the theme preference stored in your browser is the only data saved locally.
