Overtime Pay Calculator

Calculate overtime pay at daytime, night, rest-day and holiday rates.

Overtime multipliers

What are the standard multipliers?

Under Ethiopian labour guidance, overtime is typically paid at 1.5× the hourly rate for daytime, 1.75× for night work, 2× on weekly rest days and 2.5× on public holidays. Confirm with your employer or the labour proclamation.

Sources, effective date & review

Multipliers follow Article 68 of Ethiopia’s Labour Proclamation No. 1156/2019.
