Privacy Policy

No accounts, no tracking, no data collection.

Privacy at a glance

EthioSync is built so that nothing you type ever leaves your device. All tools run 100% client-side in your browser — there is no server-side processing of your inputs, no analytics on tool content, and no accounts.

What we store

  • Theme preference — a single local-storage value ("light" or "dark") so the site remembers your choice.

That is the only information stored, and it never leaves your browser.

What we do not do

  • No tracking cookies or fingerprinting.
  • No collection of names, dates, incomes or any text entered into tools.
  • No sale or sharing of data — there is no data to share.
